## Full description
Turn what's already in your kitchen into dinner — and drinks.

**Hearth & Hand** is a warm, modern-rustic recipe and cocktail organizer with an AI Kitchen &
Bar Muse at its heart. Tell it what's in your fridge and liquor cabinet, and it composes real,
fully-written recipes around your ingredients — filtered by your diet, your clock and your skill.

**THE MUSE**
• Type or tap the ingredients and spirits you have on hand
• Filter by Food vs. Drinks, prep time, dietary needs (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, keto,
  dairy-free, nut-free) and skill level
• One tap on **Surprise Me** for instant inspiration
• Every recipe card: title, prep & cook time, difficulty, categorized ingredient list,
  step-by-step instructions, and suggested pairings — wine, cocktails, zero-proof or sides

**MY SHELF**
• A visual library of everything you've saved
• Filter by All, Food, Drinks, or Favorites
• Search by title, ingredient or tag; filter by mood tags like *smoky*, *fresh*, *cozy*
• Edit any recipe, mark favorites, delete with confidence

**SMART BASKET**
• One tap on "Add Ingredients to Basket" sends every ingredient to your shopping list
• Items auto-group by aisle: Produce, Dairy, Pantry & Dry Goods, Spirits & Liquids
• Add your own items, check things off as you shop, clear crossed-off items in one tap

**COOK & MIX MODE**
• A full-screen, distraction-free step reader with big, glanceable text
• Tap steps to complete them; a progress bar and celebration mark the finish
• Works the same for simmering dal and stirring a martini

**DESIGNED LIKE A WELL-KEPT KITCHEN**
Warm creams, soft sage, slate and clay — serif display type, generous whitespace, and an
interface that feels like a favorite cutting board. Bottom navigation on phones, a sleek
dashboard layout on desktop.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Hearth & Hand works fully offline. Your recipes, favorites and shopping list live only on your
device — no account, no tracking, no data collection. Ever.

## Privacy — App Privacy / Data Safety
**Data Not Collected.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share any data. All recipes,
favorites and shopping list state are stored locally on the device using browser/App WebView
storage and are never sent to a server.

## Privacy policy (short)
Hearth & Hand stores all user content (recipes, favorites, shopping list) exclusively on the
user's device. The developer receives no data. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are
included. Deleting the app removes all data.
